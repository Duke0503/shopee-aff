# D2 (Shopee's part of a commission is capped at 40,000 dong per order)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Approved by: delegated to Claude by the project owner, who asked for the recommended option at every question. The owner may overturn it.

## Context

Carried over from the live system (docs/reference/v1-handover.vi.md).

## Decision

Shopee's part of a commission is capped at 40,000 dong per order. The shop's XTRA part is not capped.

## Rationale

Checked on 18 of 18 products. Forgetting the cap promised almost three times the real amount.
