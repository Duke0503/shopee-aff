# D29 (no deals or vouchers)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Approved by: the project owner, in the design session

## Decision

The platform has no deals, featured products or food-and-drink vouchers. The Deals context, the
`/topdeal` command, the customer Deals page and the admin Deals screen are removed.

## Rationale

The requirement no longer exists. Fewer surfaces keep the customer website simple.

## Consequences

- PRD FR-28 is withdrawn; FR-20 no longer lists /topdeal.
- The live tables `featured_deals` and `fnb_vouchers` are not imported at cutover.
- Deal photos may still be used as sample product images in mockups.
