# D9 (Customer chat runs on one personal Zalo account at a human pace)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Approved by: delegated to Claude by the project owner, who asked for the recommended option at every question. The owner may overturn it.

## Context

Carried over from the live system (docs/reference/v1-handover.vi.md).

## Decision

Customer chat runs on one personal Zalo account at a human pace. No bulk messages and no bulk friend requests.

## Rationale

The Zalo Bot API is gone since 2026-09-25. A locked account cuts contact with every customer at once.
